Support

Having a problem or have an idea? Write to msakaryawork@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed in your phone's Settings → Notifications → Patico. In the app, you can pick the reminder time under Settings → Reminders. Reminders are only scheduled for records that have a "next due" date.

I took care of something. How do I mark it?

Tap "Done" on that row on the Today screen or on your pet's page. A new record of the same type opens with the next date suggested; saving it closes the old reminder.

Are the suggested dates right?

Suggestions are general intervals for adult animals: vaccines 1 year, deworming 3 months, flea & tick 1 month. Puppy and kitten schedules and product durations can differ; you can always change the date to follow your vet's advice.

I'm switching phones. What happens to my data?

Your data is stored only on your phone. On the old phone, use Settings → "Save backup" and save the file to iCloud Drive or Google Drive. On the new phone, use Settings → "Restore from backup" and pick that file.

How do I change the language or theme?

In the app, go to Settings → Appearance to pick the language (Türkçe / English) and the theme (light / dark). "System" follows your phone's setting.

What happens if I delete a pet?

The pet and all of its records are deleted. This cannot be undone; save a backup first if you need one.
