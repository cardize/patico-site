Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

In short: Patico does not collect any personal data. There is no account, server, advertising or analytics. What you enter is stored only on your phone and never reaches us.

What does the app store?

The following information you enter is stored only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • Your pets' details: name, species, breed, date of birth, sex, neutered status and an optional microchip number
  • Health records: vaccines, deworming and flea & tick treatments, weight, vet visits, clinic name, cost and notes
  • Your settings: reminder time, language and theme

This information is not sent to us or to third parties. Deleting the app removes all of it from your phone.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Their content is created on your phone and is not sent to any server. You can turn notification permission off at any time in your phone's Settings app.

Backups

"Save backup" writes all your data to a single file on your phone and opens the share sheet. You decide where to save it (for example iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Files); we never receive or see this file. Those services store the file under their own privacy policies.

Children

Patico is a general-purpose app for pet owners and is not specifically directed at children. The app does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the changes on this page together with the date of the update.
